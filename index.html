LinchStone Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy covers the LinchStone phone app and LinchStone Helper for Windows.

No account, no LinchStone server

There is no LinchStone account and no LinchStone server. The phone talks only to LinchStone Helper on a PC you control, on your home network or over a tunnel you set up (Tailscale, Headscale or WireGuard). The phone doesn't run the models, and the PC must be on and awake.

What goes to your PC

Chat text, image prompts, photos you attach or take with the camera, and file browsing go to that PC. They are not sent to LinchStone. Local tools (LM Studio, Ollama, ComfyUI and Amuse) stay on your PC.

Cloud tools

If you add a cloud tool and run a step that uses it, the helper on your PC sends that step's text, and a photo if the step needs one, to the provider you chose (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI, Mistral or OpenRouter). Nothing is sent until you run the step. API keys are stored only on the PC, encrypted with Windows DPAPI, and are never sent to LinchStone. The phone keeps no copy.

Pairing

Pairing keys are stored on the phone and the PC. Unpair, or erase phone data in Settings, to remove the pairing key and the phone's copy of your data. Files on the PC stay until you delete them there.

Camera and microphone

LinchStone uses the camera only when you tap the camera button, to take a single photo. It never records video and never uses the camera in the background. You can also choose a photo from your gallery instead.

A photo you take or choose is sent only to your own PC. The developer of LinchStone never receives or sees your photos. On the phone, the app keeps only a temporary copy in its private storage while sending it; photos you take are not added to your gallery. On the PC, the helper holds the photo in memory for the tool you picked. If that tool is an image tool such as ComfyUI, the tool may save a copy in its own folders on your PC, as it does for any image you give it.

The optional microphone button uses Android's speech recognizer to turn speech into text. LinchStone does not receive or store the audio. Depending on your phone, Android's speech recognizer may be provided by Google or your phone's maker under their own privacy terms.

Update checks

The helper can tell you about new releases of your tools and new cloud models. While "Online checks" is on (it is on by default; switch it off in Settings → General → Tool updates), the helper on your PC makes plain requests to public release pages (such as GitHub) and asks each cloud provider you added for its list of models, using your key. These requests contain no prompts, chats, photos or files. Local-only mode stops them too.

Choices kept only on the phone

A few choices are kept only on the phone: your settings, the keepers folder you picked, which finished jobs you cleared, and which "What's new" notes you have seen. They are not sent anywhere.

Analytics

An on-phone analytics switch is off by default. If you turn it on, it only counts which screens you open, and those counts stay on the phone.

Purchases

LinchStone offers one-time purchases through Google Play (no subscription). LinchStone stores only which purchase you own, so it can apply your tool and chain limits. It never sees your payment details.

Reports you send

You can report AI-generated content (long-press a reply or image and choose Report, or use Settings → General → About → Report AI content), or contact us. This opens your own email app with a draft addressed to linchstone.dev@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use reports only to improve the app and to respond to you.

Children

LinchStone is not intended for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
